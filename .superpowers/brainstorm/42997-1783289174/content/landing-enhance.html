Landing Page Enhancement Concepts

Maintaining green/emerald theme. Which direction appeals?

A

Polished Pro

Subtle scroll-triggered fade-in/slide-up animations on each section. Floating particles in hero. Smooth number counters in stats section. Gradient text reveals on headings. Keeps current structure, adds motion polish.

B

Bold & Immersive

Full-width animated hero with rolling Kenya landscape silhouette. Parallax depth layers in hero. Animated stat cards that count up on scroll. Feature cards with hover 3D tilt effect. Animated testimonial carousel. More dramatic but same color palette.

C

Balanced Lively

Hero with subtle animated gradient + floating mockup device (phone/laptop). Sections fade in on scroll. Feature cards staggered entrance. Stats count up. Testimonials auto-rotate. Interactive FAQ. Adds life without being over-the-top. My recommendation.
